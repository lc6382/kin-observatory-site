Coverage & how the rankings work

This index groups the latest discovery results by their displayed @handle, ignoring letter case. Anonymous and missing creator labels are excluded, because they do not identify a single creator. Each portfolio contains only Kins in the current tracked set, not every Kin an account has published. Kins that leave the set retain their history on the main tracker.

Totals are the published lifetime counters of those current Kins. Hidden interaction counters are excluded and their coverage is shown. Interactions per Kin uses only Kins with visible interactions. Interactions per creation uses those same Kins in both parts of the ratio. These measures do not count unique users.

Growth is calculated for each Kin separately, then summed. A Kin must be present throughout the selected window with valid, nondecreasing counters, no recorded creator change and no gap longer than 18 hours. Earlier rows without a creator label can be matched by share code. New entries never contribute their lifetime totals as new growth. Partial portfolios show the number of contributing Kins; choose “Only full coverage” to exclude them from growth rankings.

The single-update period compares the two latest observations. The daily period uses a baseline within six hours of a day earlier; longer periods use a baseline up to 18 hours before the requested start. “Since first snapshot” uses the same first observation for everyone, when coverage was only 200 Kins. Daily rates divide by the actual elapsed time. No sufficient baseline means a dash, not zero. These tables use recorded counters; delays in Kindroid’s updates can affect short windows.

Top 100 means discovery positions 1–100, which reflect factors beyond raw interactions. The cards above use the current total, a fixed seven-day growth window with full interaction coverage, and top-100 presence. Featured status and judgments about inauthentic activity are not inferred.
