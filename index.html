How these numbers work

Discovery rank is the order of the first 1000 results returned by a search for “...”. It is not a ranking by raw interaction total. Discovery ordering also reflects update recency and model selection.

Coverage expanded from 200 to 1,000 kins on September 6, 2026. Newly included kins build history from their first observation. Lifetime totals are the exact counters shown by Kindroid. Kindroid hides interactions below 10 creations; these show as —, are excluded from interaction growth calculations, and are omitted from the displayed aggregate. Gains and daily rates cover only our observation history. Last 12 hours compares the latest two observations (one update). Last 24 hours uses the observation nearest a day earlier, within six hours; the exact observed interval is shown. A 7-day, 30-day or yearly metric requires a baseline near that period's start and continuous observations; the first version treats gaps longer than 18 hours as missing. Actual elapsed time is used for daily rates.

Interactions per day per creation = interactions gained ÷ elapsed days ÷ time-weighted average creation count. This is not a count of unique active users. Percentage growth is relative to the starting total and can be large for small starting counts. Counter decreases are excluded from growth calculations.

Kins that leave the current 1000 retain their old history, but receive no individual follow-up searches. If they return, observation resumes. A spike alone does not establish automated or inauthentic activity.

Collection targets 5:45 AM and PM Pacific time, with limited retries. GitHub schedules can be delayed. The collection status above distinguishes a manually captured baseline from verified automatic operation.
